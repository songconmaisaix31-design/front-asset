# R2-MOT — Ground sky movement

Selected source: the one useful official demo, `https://stellarium-web.org/`, captured in isolated context `R2-MOT-ground` at 1440×900 DPR 1. The raw 43.84-second WebM is one continuous browser session: the official canvas initially has a visible ground/horizon silhouette, then a single upward pointer drag moves it out of frame. Start and end screenshots have matching DOM/CSS captures; the canvas was 1140×900 at x=300 because the left official navigation panel was open.

This is evidence for genuine upward movement until ground exits, not a composite. The service's default date/time was daylight, so the end frame contains Sun/Moon/planet objects rather than stars taking over; P0-E's stronger stellar-takeover condition remains unproven. No login was used, and raw/clean HTML was not retained; these are explicit capture gaps rather than inferred behavior.

Acquisition: public official browser observation. Evidence type: aligned same-session PNG/DOM/CSS plus original unmodified WebM. Usage: reference-only and local-only until redistribution rights are independently verified.
